Baby Brainiac

$BABYBRAIN — a token themed on the fastest wiring job in nature: the first year of a human brain. The page centers on one animated illustration of the newborn stepping reflex.

The interactive demonstration

Hold a newborn upright with its feet on a surface and the spinal cord generates reciprocal stepping — a reflex present at birth that disappears by about two months as higher cortical pathways mature. Below is an interactive scripted animation of that reflex (not a biological simulation or physical recording). Toggle the three states to see the leg motion adapt:

Sources for the biology

  • ~100 billion neurons at birth, with ~250,000 neurons formed per minute before birth — NCBI, Discovering the Brain
  • A million+ synapses formed per second in early childhood — BabyCenter and University Hospitals
  • 275–300 bones at birth fusing into the adult 206 — Cleveland Clinic
  • Newborn stepping reflex fades by ~2 months — AAP / HealthyChildren.org

These figures are published estimates cited from their sources, not measurements made by this project.
